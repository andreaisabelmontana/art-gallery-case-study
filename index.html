02 / FROM BRIEF TO SCHEMA

Relational model

Primary keys identify records. Foreign keys link records; junction tables store each many-to-many relationship once.

ARTIST 1 ───── 0..* ARTWORK
ARTWORK 0..* ───── 0..* ART GROUP
CUSTOMER 0..* ───── 0..* ARTIST
CUSTOMER 0..* ───── 0..* ART GROUP
Case requirementImplementationIntegrity rule
Artist: identifier, name, birthplace, birthdate, styleartistUnique primary key; birthplace, date and style may be NULL when unpublished.
Artwork: year, ID, title, type, price and artistartwork → artistExactly one artist per artwork, valid year, and non-negative COP price when known. Medium is published metadata; broad art type is a case-study classification.
Art group: code, descriptive name; multiple groups per workart_group + artwork_groupComposite key disallows duplicate membership; provenance distinguishes portfolio collections from illustrative extra memberships.
Customer: ID, unique name, address and lifetime spendcustomerName uniqueness follows the case study. SQLite NOCASE applies to ASCII; production Unicode matching requires a defined collation. Lifetime spend is a stored non-negative COP total, not inferred from current art prices.
Customer likes artists and groupscustomer_artist + customer_groupTwo independent junctions prevent duplicate likes and preserve zero-or-more preferences.
