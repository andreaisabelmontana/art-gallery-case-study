04 / Collector records

Synthetic IDs, names, addresses, lifetime spend and preferences. These are not Andrea’s customers or sales.

05 / Relational schema

artist       1 ─── 0..* artwork
artwork      1 ─── 0..* artwork_group   0..* ─── 1 art_group
customer     1 ─── 0..* customer_artist 0..* ─── 1 artist
customer     1 ─── 0..* customer_group  0..* ─── 1 art_group

Constraints: Primary keys identify records; foreign keys prevent dangling links; composite keys reject duplicate memberships or likes. Customer names are unique using SQLite’s ASCII case-insensitive comparison. Unknown facts are NULL, not zero.
